## 1. What is Fundamental Analysis?
Understanding a company's intrinsic value involves looking beyond the daily price ticker. For **Tata Power**, this means evaluating its business model (Renewables, Distribution, Transmission), its financial statements, and its growth ratios against competitors like Adani Power and NTPC.

## 2. Key Valuation Metrics for Tata Power

### **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio**
The P/E ratio answers the question: *"How much am I paying for ₹1 of profit?"*
*   **Tata Power Current P/E:** Check our [live dashboard](/stocks/TATAPOWER).
*   **Interpretation:** A high P/E relative to the sector might indicate high growth expectations (e.g., massive EV charging expansion), while a low P/E could suggest undervaluation.

### **Return on Equity (ROE)**
ROE measures efficiency. If Tata Power has an ROE of 12%, it generates ₹12 profit for every ₹100 of shareholder equity. Consistent ROE growth is a sign of a strong management moat.

### **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)**
For capital-intensive sectors like Power & Utilities, **ROCE** is critical. It accounts for debt. A healthy ROCE (>15%) means the company uses both equity and debt efficiently to generate returns.

*   **Target:** Ideally < 1.5 for power companies.
