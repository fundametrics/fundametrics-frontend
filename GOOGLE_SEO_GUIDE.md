1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter your domain: `fundametrics.in`
4. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **DNS verification** (recommended for Cloudflare)

### Step 2: Submit Sitemap
Once verified:
1. In Google Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left menu)
2. Enter sitemap URL: `https://fundametrics.in/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"

### Step 3: Request Indexing for Key Pages
Manually request indexing for important pages:
1. Go to "URL Inspection" tool
